Our story

Born from a simple question: why is good music tuition so hard to reach?

Families in Hyderabad told us the same thing again and again. They wanted their child to learn an instrument, but long traffic-filled commutes, fixed batch timings and large classes made it hard to stay consistent.

Raga House began as an answer to that. We bring experienced teachers to the learner, so the lesson happens in a familiar space, at a convenient time, with full attention on one student.

Today we support learners of every age, from first-time children to adults picking up an instrument they always loved. Our focus has never changed: patient teaching, honest feedback and music that is enjoyable to practise.

  • Small, personal and consistent, never a crowd
  • Beginners and returning learners are equally welcome
  • Western and Indian instruments under one roof

Our mission

Make every instrument lesson personal and joyful

We bring skilled, caring teachers to your home so children and adults can learn guitar, piano, violin or tabla without the commute, crowded classes or pressure. Every lesson is one-to-one and shaped around the learner’s level, interests and goals.

  • One-to-one lessons at your doorstep
  • Teachers matched to the learner and instrument
  • Simple practice guidance after every lesson
Our vision

A Hyderabad where every home can learn an instrument

We imagine a city where high-quality instrument tuition is convenient and personal: where a child can learn piano after school, a teenager can build guitar skills, an adult can start violin, and families can discover the rhythm of tabla at home.

  • Instrument tuition that fits family routines
  • Guitar, piano, violin and tabla for different goals
  • Skills for confidence, enjoyment and exams

Talk to us
What age can a child start?

Many children begin piano or tabla around age 5, and guitar or violin from about 7. Your teacher will advise during the trial lesson.

Do we need to own an instrument first?

No. We recommend waiting until after the trial. Your teacher will suggest a suitable instrument and size for your budget.

How long is each lesson?

Lessons are usually 45 to 60 minutes, once or twice a week, at a time that fits your family’s routine.

Can we change the teacher if it isn’t a good fit?

Yes. If the match doesn’t feel right, tell us and we will arrange another teacher at no extra cost.

Do you prepare students for exams?

Yes. Teachers can prepare learners for graded music exams, school performances and competitions.
