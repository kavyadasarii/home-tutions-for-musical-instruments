Before you book

Questions parents usually ask

Can't find what you need here? Reach out and a real person will answer.

Do we need to already own an instrument?

For piano and guitar, most families use what they already have at home. For tabla and violin, the teacher brings a spare instrument to the trial so you can decide before buying anything.

What if the weekly time stops working for us?

Message your teacher directly — most reschedules are sorted within the same week. If a permanent time change is needed, we'll help find a new slot that fits.

Is there a minimum term or contract?

No. Lessons run week to week and can be paused or stopped with a week's notice — the trial lesson has no obligation attached at all.

How are teachers chosen for a match?

We look at the student's age and level, the teacher's home locality, and the requested time slot, then confirm availability before anyone visits.

Which areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

30+ localities today, including Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli, Kondapur and Secunderabad. Tell us your area on the trial form and we'll confirm coverage.
