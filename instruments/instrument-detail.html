Ages 7+
Most-picked first instrument

Guitar

Acoustic or electric, from a student's very first chord to playing full songs. Lessons move at a pace that keeps beginners motivated without skipping the fundamentals.

Ages 7+ 45–60 min sessions Acoustic or electric One-on-one, at home

Good fit for: kids who already hum along to songs, and teens who eventually want to jam or join a band.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

Chords & strumming

Open chord shapes, changing cleanly between them, and strumming patterns for popular songs.

Fingerstyle basics

Introductory fingerpicking patterns once strumming and chord changes feel comfortable.

Reading tabs & charts

How to read tablature and chord charts so a student can learn new songs independently.

First full songs

Most students are playing a complete, simplified song by the end of the second month.

Ages 5+
Youngest starting age

Piano

Classical technique built on your own keyboard or upright, with a syllabus that can follow ABRSM grading if a student wants to sit exams later on.

Ages 5+ 45–60 min sessions 61-key keyboard or upright needed ABRSM track optional

Good fit for: younger children starting their first instrument, and families with a keyboard or piano already at home.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

Hand position & technique

Correct posture and finger technique from lesson one, so bad habits never get a chance to form.

Sight-reading & theory

Note reading, rhythm and the basics of music theory, built into every lesson gradually.

Scales, chords & pieces

Major and minor scales, simple chord progressions, and short graded pieces to perform.

ABRSM exam prep

An optional exam-prep track from Grade 1 onward, for families who want a formal benchmark.

Ages 6+

Violin

Posture, bowing and ear training from the very first lesson, taught in either the Carnatic or Western classical tradition — or a blend of both once the fundamentals are solid.

Ages 6+ 45–60 min sessions Spare violin for lesson one Carnatic or Western

Good fit for: families drawn to Carnatic music, and kids who show a good ear for pitch early on.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

Posture & bow control

How to hold the violin and bow correctly, and produce a clean, even tone on open strings.

Ear training

Pitch-matching and interval drills built into every lesson from the very start.

Carnatic or Western scales

Sarali varisai and Western major scales, taught in the tradition your family prefers.

First compositions

Simple geethams or short Western pieces once bowing and pitch are dependable.

Pricing

Ages 6+
Easiest to pick up young

Tabla

Rhythm and taal built through call-and-response drills that make timing feel like play — often the easiest instrument for a young child to take to straight away.

Ages 6+ 45–60 min sessions Spare tabla set for lesson one Floor seating space

Good fit for: energetic kids, and as a natural first instrument for very young learners.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

Basic bols & strokes

The foundational hand strokes and spoken bols used to build every rhythm that follows.

Taal counting

Keeping time in Teentaal and Keherwa, counted out loud before it's played on the drums.

Call-and-response drills

Playful rhythm games with the teacher that build timing without feeling like drilling.

Playing to music

Accompanying simple recorded compositions once basic taals feel steady.
