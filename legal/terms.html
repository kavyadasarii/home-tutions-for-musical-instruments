02 Services we provide

Raga House connects students and families in Hyderabad with independent, vetted music teachers who deliver weekly, one-on-one guitar, piano, violin, and tabla lessons at the student's home. Raga House handles teacher matching, scheduling, payments, and lesson quality — teachers are responsible for the delivery of individual lessons.

03 Booking & scheduling

Weekly lesson slots are requested through the student dashboard or by contacting our team, and are confirmed once a matched teacher accepts the request. Confirmed slots repeat automatically at the same day and time each week unless rescheduled or cancelled by either party.

  • Slot requests are typically confirmed within one business day.
  • A slot is considered "yours" only once it shows as Confirmed, not merely Requested.
  • We reserve the right to reassign a teacher if the original teacher becomes unavailable, with advance notice where possible.

04 Free trial classes

New students are entitled to one complimentary trial class per instrument, subject to teacher availability in your locality. Trial classes do not obligate you to continue with paid lessons, and no payment details are required to book one.

05 Fees & payments

Lesson fees are billed monthly in advance and are shown to you before you confirm a booking. Payment is due by the date shown on your Payments page; lessons may be paused if a fee remains unpaid more than 7 days after its due date.

Fees are set per instrument, teacher, and lesson length, and may be revised with at least 30 days' notice before your next billing cycle.

06 Cancellations & rescheduling

  • Students may cancel or reschedule a single lesson with at least 24 hours' notice at no charge.
  • Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed lessons without notice, are billed as normal and are not eligible for a makeup class.
  • A weekly slot itself can be cancelled at any time from the Book a Lesson page; this stops future recurring bookings but does not refund already-billed lessons.
  • If a teacher needs to cancel, we will offer a makeup lesson or a credit toward your next invoice.

07 Conduct & safety

Raga House teachers undergo background checks and an audition before being matched with students. In turn, we ask families to provide a safe, quiet space for lessons and to have a parent or guardian reasonably available at home during sessions with minors.

We take reports of unsafe or inappropriate conduct — by a teacher or a student's household — seriously, and may suspend or end an arrangement immediately if such conduct is reported or observed.
