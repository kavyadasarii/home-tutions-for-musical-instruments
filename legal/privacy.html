01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Raga House ("we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our website, book a trial class, or use the student dashboard. By using our Services, you agree to the practices described here.

02 Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Account & booking detailsParent/student name, phone number, email, home address or locality, instrument preference
Lesson activityBooking history, attendance, practice assignments, teacher notes, lesson recordings
Payment informationBilling amount, payment status, and a reference from our payment processor — we do not store full card numbers
Technical dataIP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited on our website

03 How we use your information

  • To match you with a suitable teacher and schedule lessons
  • To process payments and send receipts
  • To share practice notes, assignments, and recordings through your dashboard
  • To send booking confirmations, reminders, and service updates
  • To respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • To improve our website and Services, and to keep them secure

04 Cookies & similar technologies

Our website uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and to remember preferences such as light/dark theme and text direction. We may also use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work correctly if essential cookies are disabled.

05 How we share information

We share the minimum information necessary with:

  • Your matched teacher — contact details, address, and lesson preferences needed to deliver lessons
  • Payment processors — to process fees securely
  • Service providers — such as hosting, email, and analytics providers who process data on our behalf
  • Authorities — where required by law, or to protect the safety of a student or teacher

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

06 Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — including encrypted connections and access controls — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07 Children's privacy

Many of our students are minors. Accounts and bookings for a minor must be created and managed by a parent or legal guardian, who consents to this policy on the student's behalf. We collect only the information needed to deliver lessons safely and do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts independently.

08 Data retention

We retain account, booking, and payment records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution needs. Lesson recordings are retained on your dashboard for a rolling period and can be deleted on request.

09 Your rights & choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing. You can update most details yourself from the Profile page, or contact us to make a request.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the dashboard or by email at least 14 days before they take effect.
