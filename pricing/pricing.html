Questions

Pricing, answered

What's included in a lesson?

Every visit is a full 60-minute one-on-one lesson at your home, plus a short practice note the teacher leaves behind covering what to work on before the next visit. Teachers bring sheet music and, for tabla and violin, a spare instrument for your first trial class.

Can I switch instruments or plans later?

Yes. You can change instrument, move between Starter, Standard and Premium, or add a second instrument at any time — changes apply from your next billing cycle.

Is there a refund or pause option?

You can pause a plan for school holidays or travel with two days' notice, and cancel anytime with no exit fee. Unused lessons in a paid month carry forward for up to four weeks.

Do you offer a sibling discount?

Yes — a second child in the same household gets 15% off their own plan, regardless of instrument or plan tier.

How do I pay?

Monthly or quarterly, by UPI, card or net banking. Quarterly billing saves 10% compared to paying monthly.
