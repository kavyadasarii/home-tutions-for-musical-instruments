The full process

Five steps to your first lesson

Most families go from enquiry to their first trial class within three to five days. Here's exactly what happens at each stage.

1

Share your details

Fill the short trial form or call us with the student's age, the instrument they want to learn, and your locality in Hyderabad. Takes under two minutes.

2

Get matched with a teacher

We shortlist a teacher based on instrument, age group, teaching style and distance from your home, then call to confirm a convenient day and time.

3

Attend a free trial class

The teacher visits at the scheduled time with any equipment needed for the trial, gets a feel for the student's level, and answers any questions you have.

4

Settle into weekly lessons

Happy with the trial? Pick a plan and lock in a weekly slot. The same teacher returns each week, building continuity instead of starting over each time.

5

Track progress every week

After each session, the teacher leaves a short practice note covering what was learned, what to practice before the next visit, and what's coming up next.

What to expect

Your first trial lesson, explained.

There's no obligation and nothing to prepare. The teacher arrives with everything needed for the trial and spends the full session getting to know the student.

Expect a short conversation about musical interests and goals, a simple assessment of current ability (even if that's none at all), and a first taste of actually playing. By the end, you'll have a clear sense of the teacher's style and a recommended starting point for regular lessons.

Scheduling

Lessons that flex around your week.

Life in Hyderabad rarely runs on a fixed clock — exams, travel and long workdays all get in the way. Our scheduling is built to bend around that, not fight it.

Pick a recurring weekly slot that suits your household, and the same teacher keeps returning at that time. Need to shift a lesson because of a school event or a family trip? Message your coordinator at least 24 hours ahead and we'll reschedule within the same week wherever possible, at no extra cost. If a teacher is ever unavailable due to illness or travel, we arrange a qualified substitute rather than simply cancelling the week, so momentum is never lost.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the trial class really free?

Yes. The first trial lesson is completely free, with no obligation to continue. You only choose a paid weekly plan if you and the student are happy after the trial.

Do I need to own an instrument before starting?

Not for the trial. For violin and tabla, teachers bring a spare instrument to the trial class. For guitar and piano, we'll guide you on an affordable starter instrument once you decide to continue.

Can I change my weekly lesson time later?

Yes. Give your coordinator at least 24 hours' notice and we'll shift that week's lesson, or help you move to a new permanent weekly slot if your routine changes.

What happens if the teacher can't make it one week?

We arrange a qualified substitute teacher wherever possible so the lesson still happens. If that isn't possible, the session is rescheduled within the same week at no extra cost.

Which areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

We currently teach in over 30 localities across Hyderabad, including Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Gachibowli and Kondapur. Share your locality on the trial form and we'll confirm coverage.

How do I track what my child is learning?

After every lesson, the teacher leaves a short written practice note — what was covered, what to practice before the next visit, and what's coming up next. No app or login needed.
