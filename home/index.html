Beyond the lesson

Practice that fits real family life.

Lessons are only half the picture. What happens between visits — the ten minutes before school, the half hour after dinner — is where progress is actually made.

Every teacher leaves behind a short practice note after each session: what to work on, how long to spend on it, and what to expect at the next visit. No apps to install, no logins to remember — just a clear plan a parent can glance at on a busy evening. Over a term, those small notes add up to a student who knows exactly what they're aiming for, and a parent who can see the progress without having to ask.
